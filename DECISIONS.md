# DECISIONS.md

## Why CSV Uploads?

Enterprise onboarding usually starts with exports rather than direct integrations.

CSV exports are common for:

- SAP ECC
- Utility portals
- Concur exports

## Why Sync Processing?

Synchronous ingestion reduced implementation complexity and simplified debugging.

## Why Hardcoded Emission Factors?

The assignment focused on ingestion architecture rather than emissions science integrations.
